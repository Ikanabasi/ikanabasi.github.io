Overview

The short version of what this project is and why I worked on it.

The use case came from a local media company in Sault Ste. Marie. The public-facing chatbot needs useful information about community resources, but the hard part is keeping that information current and verified.

My focus would be the data-collection side: discovering sources, extracting resource details, checking evidence, structuring the result and preserving enough source information that the chatbot can answer from something traceable.

The problem

What needed to be solved before thinking about tools or models.

What made it difficult

  • Community information changes frequently and may be spread across many websites.
  • Search results are not the same thing as verified data.
  • A public chatbot needs current, structured information and source traceability.

What I built / designed

  • Defined the research and verification problem the system needs to solve.
  • Outlined an agentic workflow for discovery, extraction, verification and structured storage.
  • Identified source traceability and freshness as core requirements rather than optional features.

How it works

A simplified view of the system or workflow.

Public web→Discovery agent→Extract details→Verify sources→Structured resource store→RAG / chatbot
